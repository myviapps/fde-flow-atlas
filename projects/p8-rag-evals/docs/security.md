Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique to each system. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN and all production systems.

Lock your laptop screen whenever you step away. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Report suspected phishing emails with the Report Phishing button in your mail client. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the security team within 1 hour.
