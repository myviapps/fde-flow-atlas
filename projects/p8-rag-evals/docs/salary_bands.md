---
title: Salary bands (confidential)
acl: hr
---
Salary bands are reviewed every March using market data from two compensation surveys.

The band for level L3 engineers is 95,000 to 125,000 dollars. The band for level L4 engineers is 120,000 to 155,000 dollars.

Offers above the top of a band require approval from the VP of People and the CFO.
