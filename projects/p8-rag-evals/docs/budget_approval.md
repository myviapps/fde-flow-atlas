---
title: Budget approval (finance)
acl: finance
---
Purchases under 1,000 dollars need approval from your manager only. Purchases from 1,000 to 10,000 dollars need approval from the department head.

Any purchase over 10,000 dollars requires approval from the CFO before a purchase order is issued.

Budgets are set each November for the following fiscal year, which starts on February 1.
