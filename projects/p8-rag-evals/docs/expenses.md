---
title: Expense reimbursement
acl: all
---
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase date using the expense tool. Reports submitted later than 90 days are not reimbursed.

An itemized receipt is required for every expense over 25 dollars. Alcohol and personal entertainment are never reimbursable.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after your manager approves the report.
