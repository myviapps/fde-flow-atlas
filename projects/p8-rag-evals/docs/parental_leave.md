---
title: Parental leave
acl: all
---
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave.

Parental leave can be taken any time within 12 months of the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child.

Tell HR at least 30 days before your planned leave start date when possible, so your team can plan coverage.
