title: Business travel
acl: all
---
Book all flights and hotels through the company travel portal so that we can locate travelers in an emergency.

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.

The hotel limit is 250 dollars per night in most cities and 350 dollars per night in high-cost cities. The daily meal allowance while traveling is 60 dollars.
