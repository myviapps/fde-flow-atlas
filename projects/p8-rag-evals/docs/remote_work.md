---
title: Remote work
acl: all
---
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's agreement. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone.

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 500 dollars for a chair, desk or monitor. Submit the receipt through the expense tool within 60 days of starting remote work.

Working from another country for more than 14 days requires written approval from HR because of tax and visa rules.
