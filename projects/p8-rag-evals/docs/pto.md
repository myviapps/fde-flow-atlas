---
title: Paid time off
acl: all
---
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO accrues monthly at about 1.67 days per month, starting on the first day of employment.

Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines within five business days.

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Days above that limit expire on December 31.
